  <p>
    Most RL projects rely on internal game variables (like player position or object distance), 
    but in the real world, that data isn't always available.
    This project started from a simple curiosity:
  </p>

 
<p style="text-align: center;"><strong><em>Can an AI learn just from visual input, like humans do?</em></strong></p>


🌟<strong>Design Rationale</strong>

  🎯Reward Mechanism
  <ul>
    <li>Penalty on game over: encourages survival.</li>
    <li>Small positive reward per frame: promotes long-term performance.</li>
  </ul>

  🖼️Visual Preprocessing
  <ul>
    <li>Edge detection: strips out background clutter.</li>
    <li>Removal unnecessary element (score, cloud, etc) to further emphasis essential element.</li>
  </ul>


🧠 AI Model & Training
  <ul>
    <li>CNN: ideal for extracting spatial patterns from image input.</li>
    <li>Experience Replay: enables stable training from diverse past experiences.</li>
  </ul>
