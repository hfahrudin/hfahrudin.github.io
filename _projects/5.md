Facex is a lightweight emotion classifier trained on various emotion benchmark datasets and tested on edge devices. The model originates from my <a href="https://hfahrudin.github.io/projects/4/" target="_blank">AI Agent Solutions</a>. (Note: The model was trained some time ago. While it performs well, newer advancements in the field may offer improved results.)

The goal of this effort is to make my trained model easily usable and inference-ready for anyone. I believe the potential applications of this work span across various domains, and I am excited to see how others might extend and build upon it.
