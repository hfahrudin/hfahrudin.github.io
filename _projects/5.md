<strong>Facex</strong> is a lightweight emotion classifier trained on various emotion benchmark datasets and tested on edge devices. The model originates from my work on <a href="https://hfahrudin.github.io/projects/11/" target="_blank">Facial Expression Detector</a>. (<em>Note: The model was trained some time ago. While it performs well, newer advancements in the field may offer improved results.</em>)

The goal of this effort is to make my trained model easily usable and inference-ready for anyone. I believe the potential applications of this work span across various domains, and I am excited to see how others might extend and build upon it.
