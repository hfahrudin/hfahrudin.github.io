<ol>
  <li>While <a href="https://github.com/Mozilla-Ocho/llamafile" target="_blank">Llamafile</a> (or similar lightweight frameworks) is quite straightforward to use, <strong>dockerization</strong> provides much greater control over deployment and environment management.</li>
  <li>In most real-world applications, you will need a <strong>separate module to handle incoming queries</strong>. When bundling both the LLM service and the query-handling logic in a Docker environment, leveraging <strong>Docker networking</strong> ensures secure and robust communication between services.</li>
  <li>Additionally, <strong>containerization unlocks seamless scalability</strong>, making it easier to scale different parts of the system independently and efficiently.</li>
</ol>
