<p>The <a href="https://www.soccer-net.org/tasks/jersey-number-recognition" target="_blank">SoccerNet Jersey Number Recognition Challenge 2023</a> required identifying soccer players' jersey numbers from short video tracklets, often spanning several hundred frames. With only <u>one week</u> to work on the challenge, there was limited time for model training and data collection, making it a particularly difficult task.</p>

<strong>⚠️ Challenges:</strong>
<ul>
    <li><u>Joining late</u> left us with only one week, limiting time for model training and data collection.</li>
    <li><u>Low-resolution, blurry images</u> made it difficult to detect and recognize jersey numbers accurately.</li>
    <li><u>Jersey numbers were visible briefly</u> in a few frames, complicating consistent recognition.</li>
    <li><u>Multiple players in tracklets</u> created challenges in distinguishing and correctly tracking the target player.</li>
</ul>

<strong>💡 Solution:</strong>
<p>Given the time limitations, we believed that a heuristic-based approachwould make the most sense, as it could offer a more controlled and efficient solution under the circumstances. We designed a <u>Heuristic-Based Tracklet Aggregation Pipeline</u> tailored to handle these challenges effectively.</p>

<strong>🛠️ Proposed Method:</strong>
<ul>
    <li><em>Pre-Processing:</em> Enhanced input resolution and filtered out blurry frames using an ESRGAN-based super-resolution model.</li>
    <li><em>Localization:</em> Applied pose estimation to detect body keypoints, helping accurately localize jersey number regions based on player orientation.</li>
    <li><em>Recognition:</em> Performed digit recognition on localized tracklet segments.</li>
    <li><em>Decision:</em> Aggregated recognition results using a <u>Multi-Step Aggregation Thresholds</u> mechanism that factors in image quality and prediction confidence to determine the final jersey number.</li>
</ul>

<strong>🎯 Result:</strong>
<ul>
    <li>Achieved <strong>75.18% accuracy</strong> on the <a href="https://www.soccer-net.org/tasks/jersey-number-recognition" target="_blank">official challange set</a>, outperforming several deep learning-based approaches on the leaderboard.</li>
    <li>Demonstrated that a carefully designed heuristic pipeline can <strong>effectively compete </strong>with learning-based methods under strict data and quality constraints.</li>
</ul>

<strong>📌 Key Takeaway:</strong>
<p>By leveraging super-resolution, pose-guided localization, and intelligent aggregation heuristics, we were able to show that an engineering-first, heuristic approach could achieve strong performance even when time and resources were limited.</p>
